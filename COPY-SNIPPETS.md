# Price & value copy snippets

Replace `{{PRODUCT}}` / `{{PRICE}}` / `{{BUNDLE}}`. Soft tone only.

## Charm / clarity

- **$12:** "A complete {{PRODUCT}} -- priced to ship today, not to gatekeep."
- **$19:** "One kit, one job: {{OUTCOME}}. {{PRICE}} once; yours to reuse."
- **$27 Bundle:** "{{A}} + {{B}} together for {{PRICE}} (less than buying both)."
- **$29:** "Full client workflow -- proposal through kickoff -- for less than one unpaid hour."

## Anchoring (honest)

- "Most freelancers buy Invoice ($12) and Cashflow ($19) separately -- Bundle is $27."
- "Client Ops is $29; pair with Cashflow ($19) when you're ready to track profit too."

## Objection softeners

- "Not a course. Templates + checklists you can use the same day."
- "MIT / reusable docs -- no subscription."
- "If it's not useful, use the platform refund window -- no drama."

## What not to say

- Fake countdown timers.
- "Was $97" with no history.
- Income guarantees or tax/legal advice claims.
